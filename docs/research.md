# Why this target

Research checked 2026-11-03. Presspage's [own plans page](https://presspage.com/pr-software-plans) lists Business from EUR 20,000 a year and Enterprise from EUR 35,000 a year, with the actual setup determining the price. These are vendor starting prices, not an observed customer invoice. No currency conversion is claimed.

Media relations is a new family in the rebuild registry. It is separate from tonight's curriculum and dental laboratory builds, and from generic CRM, paid campaign analytics and editorial production. Its core records are incoming journalist questions, owners, deadlines, checked statements and release embargoes.

Automated screenshots and externally sourced metrics weaken the database-only case. Prezly's current base plans did not establish a five-figure annual bill on its [pricing page](https://www.prezly.com/pricing); third-party per-user descriptions were inconsistent and were not used to manufacture a large bill.

These are editorial scores, not measured market statistics. The bill score for Presspage uses its five-figure published euro starting price without pretending it is an NZ or AU invoice. Local customer-record searches did not identify a confirmed user. No live CRM buyer was verified. The low buyer score reflects that gap. Public evidence places the tool with communications teams; no local customer claim is made.

Search results for Presspage alternatives and pricing contain [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/164876/PressPage/), [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/presspage/reviews) and comparison directories. This is evidence of a comparison-query surface, not keyword volume or guaranteed ranking. No unsupported customer complaint or savings claim is used.

## Vendor facts used

- [Plans](https://presspage.com/pr-software-plans): the annual starting prices and bundled communications functions. The page compares ownership of an internal desk with the paid suite; it does not claim every connected service has been recreated.
- [Contacts](https://help.presspage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009928559-Contacts): owned contact management, restrictions and CSV export.
- [Contact lists](https://help.presspage.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003470232-Contact-Lists): list and contact CSV exports.
- [Inquiries overview](https://help.presspage.com/hc/en-us/articles/5933790323612-Inquiries-Overview): subject, requester, organisation, assignee, status, deadline and reusable statements informed the local schema.

CSV export is documented, but these help articles do not publish a fixed column contract. The adapter supports English aliases and explicit header maps; the included sample is invented. A real export remains an acceptance step for each business, not evidence this demo claims to possess.

Presspage's licensed media directory and distribution network are outside this rebuild. The useful standalone work is an owned list and the internal enquiry and approval desk. No OAuth connection is required for that work. Public publishing, sending, crawling and regulated investor announcements are absent.
